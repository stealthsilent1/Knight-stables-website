About Us

Quality Western Horse Boarding in Moorpark

Knight Stables provides a safe, comfortable, and fully covered environment for your horse. Located on Lapeyre Road in Moorpark, our facility is built for the needs of both horse and rider.

Whether you ride western or are looking for a reliable boarding home, our well-maintained grounds and attentive care give you peace of mind knowing your horse is in good hands.

What We Offer

Facility Amenities

Everything your horse needs under one fully covered roof — designed for comfort, safety, and convenience.

Covered Arena

Spacious 75ft x 200ft covered arena for riding and training in any weather, day or night.

12x12 Covered Stalls

Generously sized inside covered stalls providing shelter, comfort, and ample room for your horse.
